## 3. The 12-month point – how to reinforce it
- **Quote Rule 7(b):** "they must be of a variety that will grow to the required height and width to conceal the unit(s) **within a twelve (12) month period**." The Rule defines the standard as *variety + time*, not instant opacity.
- **Show the variety:** plant name on the Rules' recommended list; mature size from the tag/IFAS; installation date + receipt.
- **Show the plan:** spacing, watering/fertilizing, and a commit to send updated photos at 3, 6, and 12 months (e.g., Jan 10, Apr 10, Jun 10 2027).
- **Offer an interim measure** (this is your best good-faith move): keep bins in the **garage** except collection days (Rule 14(f) expressly allows garage storage) until the plants fill in; photograph it.
- **Ask for approval "with conditions"** (the ARC form has that box): conditioned on the 12-month growth check, instead of a denial or removal order.
- **Highlight timing:** plants were in the ground ~6 weeks before the 7/21 hearing and ~5 weeks before the hearing letter – the committee judged a plant-based method that was, by Rule, still within its growth window.
