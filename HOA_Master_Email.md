## Notes before sending
- **"[Board president]":** the name is a placeholder I never had. Your first message said you "spoke with [the] president" and emailed, so add that person's name, or delete the Cc if you'd rather keep it to Callahan.
- **Set a firm submission date.** Because you're asking first, pick a date (e.g., within a week) and keep it. That starts the ARC's 30-day clock and avoids looking like you're stalling.
- **Pavers beside the driveway:** Rule 8 requires ARC approval for changes to the driveway's "surface or size." Keep describing the pavers accurately as a small bin path and not a widening, and only keep the bracketed "inside lot lines / outside easement" statement if your survey confirms it.
- **Dead-plant replacement (Rule 17(g)):** replacing a dead plant in the same spot with the same or "appropriate pre-approved" plant needs no ARC request. Clusia is on the approved list. If the Clusia replaced dead plants in the same spot, say so, since it may help. If they're new locations, delete that bracket.
- **Rock and mulch:** Rule 17(a) requires ARC approval for "landscape changes," and the Rules recommend wood or rubber mulch, lava rock, and multi-colored river rock. Ask rather than assert, as the email does.
- **Account fix:** interest may recur ~Nov 11; if no reply on §5 in 2 business days, send it as a separate follow-up.
